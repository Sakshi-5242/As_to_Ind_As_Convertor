Act as a **Chartered Accountant, Ind AS technical expert, financial-statement preparation specialist, MCA/Companies Act compliance expert, Excel automation specialist, UI/UX designer and senior HTML5/CSS3/JavaScript developer**. Develop a professional, working, browser-based application titled:

**“AS to Ind AS Applicability & Financial Statement Conversion Assistant”**

The application is intended for **company management, finance teams and accountants preparing financial statements**, not for auditing. It will be used for companies preparing financial statements for **FY 2026–27 onwards**.

### 1. AUTHORITATIVE TECHNICAL FRAMEWORK

Build the application strictly using the **latest Ind AS notified and applicable in India for FY 2026–27**, based primarily on:

- MCA notifications under the Companies Act, 2013;
- Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015, as amended;
- latest applicable MCA amendments/notifications;
- ICAI Accounting Standards Board publications;
- ICAI Ind AS Compendium;
- ICAI Educational Material on Ind AS 101;
- ICAI Ind AS Disclosure Checklist;
- ICAI ITFG Clarifications and relevant technical guidance.

Do **not hallucinate** accounting requirements. Do not treat IFRS, IAS, ICAI exposure drafts, articles, blogs or proposed standards as notified Ind AS. Clearly distinguish **law/notification**, **ICAI guidance**, and **illustrative professional guidance**.

For FY 2026–27, incorporate only standards and amendments legally effective for that reporting period. Do not automatically incorporate proposed Ind AS 118 merely because an exposure draft exists.

### 2. IND AS APPLICABILITY ENGINE

Create an interactive questionnaire that determines whether the company is required to follow AS or Ind AS.

Capture:

- company type;
- listed/unlisted status;
- whether securities are proposed to be listed;
- net worth;
- net-worth history and relevant reference dates;
- holding/subsidiary/associate/joint-venture relationships;
- whether parent/group entities follow Ind AS;
- nature of business;
- NBFC status;
- insurance/banking/other specifically regulated status;
- comparative financial information;
- voluntary adoption, where legally permitted.

The engine must show:

**“Ind AS Applicable / Ind AS Not Applicable / Further Information Required”**

and clearly explain the rule/trigger supporting the conclusion.

Provide an **Applicability Timeline** showing the first financial year of mandatory Ind AS adoption and the corresponding transition date.

### 3. AS → IND AS TRANSITION ENGINE

If Ind AS applicability is triggered, activate a **First-Time Adoption / Conversion Workspace** based on **Ind AS 101**.

Explain:

- date of transition;
- opening Ind AS Balance Sheet;
- comparative period requirements;
- previous GAAP;
- mandatory exceptions;
- optional exemptions;
- deemed-cost options;
- reconciliation requirements.

The system should create a structured **conversion checklist** rather than blindly converting figures.

### 4. EXCEL TRIAL BALANCE UPLOAD

Provide an accountant-friendly interface to upload an **Excel Trial Balance (.xlsx/.xls)**.

Allow mapping of columns such as:

- Account Code
- Account Name
- Group
- Sub-group
- Debit
- Credit
- Closing Balance
- Previous Year Balance
- Nature

Provide automatic validation for:

- duplicate account codes;
- blank account names;
- invalid numbers;
- inconsistent signs;
- unclassified accounts.

Allow manual mapping/editing before processing.

- recognition adjustments;

- fair-value adjustments;
- impairment adjustments;
- amortised-cost adjustments;

- lease adjustments;
- employee-benefit adjustments;

- provisions/contingencies;
- borrowing-cost adjustments;
- foreign-exchange adjustments;
- related-party classification;
- consolidation adjustments;
- presentation/reclassification adjustments.

Do not make an accounting adjustment solely from the account name when additional facts are required. Instead display:

**“Management Input Required”**

with targeted questions.

**Previous GAAP → Ind AS Classification → Reason → Relevant Ind AS → Financial Statement Impact**

- financial asset categories;
- financial liabilities;
- investment property;
- PPE/intangibles;
- lease assets/liabilities;
- employee-benefit liabilities;
- provisions;
- equity versus liability classification.

Maintain two distinct outputs:

Build an **Ind AS Disclosure Checklist** linked to the converted trial balance and questionnaire.

### 9. IND AS 101 RECONCILIATION

Automatically prepare an editable reconciliation structure for:

- equity at end of comparative period;
- total comprehensive income/profit reconciliation;
- material adjustment explanations;
- tax effects;
- classification/reclassification effects.

- reclassification;

- equity impact;
- profit impact;
- disclosure checklist status;
- financial-statement mapping.

Maintain a complete **audit-style calculation trail**, but make clear that the application is an accounting preparation tool and not an audit tool.
