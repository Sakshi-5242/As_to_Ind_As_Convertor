The Problem Statement

Critical Pain Points in Indian Corporate AS-to-Ind AS Transition

Challenges faced by CFOs, accounting teams, and practitioners transitioning from Previous Indian GAAP

01

Complex Statutory Rules

MCA Rule 4 & Net Worth Ambiguity
  • Non-linear thresholds (₹250 Cr, listed equity/debt, NBFC Phase I/II).
  • Confusion connecting reference audit year to the statutory date of transition.
  • Holding, subsidiary, associate & JV group cascade triggers often missed.
  • Section 2(57) Net Worth computation disputes with statutory auditors.
02

Fragile Spreadsheets

Manual Mapping & Reconciliation Bottlenecks
  • Broken Excel formula chains during complex trial balance restatements.
  • Lack of simultaneous dual traceability between Previous GAAP and Ind AS.
  • Missing audit trail for opening balance adjustments under Ind AS 101.
  • Data confidentiality risks when uploading client financials to unvetted cloud tools.
03

Schedule III Gaps

Division II Compliance Failures
  • Omission of Statement of Changes in Equity (SOCIE Parts A & B).
  • Inadequate ICAI structured tables for Notes 1 to 14 disclosure.
  • Manual errors in Lease accounting (Ind AS 116), ECL, and Fair Value adjustments.
  • Cumbersome comparative restatements for prior financial years.
